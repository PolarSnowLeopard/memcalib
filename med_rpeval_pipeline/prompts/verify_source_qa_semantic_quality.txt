1. question_completeness

   - uncertain: the task is mostly visible but an important missing fragment may change it.

2. answer_relevance
   - pass: the answer directly addresses the central user task and the same condition, symptom, treatment, or constraint.
   - reject: it answers a different problem, ignores the main task, or is only unrelated boilerplate.
   - uncertain: some content is relevant but the central alignment is ambiguous.
   Evidence must be an exact quote from SOURCE ANSWER.

3. answer_substantiveness
   - pass: the answer provides a meaningful explanation, recommendation, clarification, warning, or next step beyond greetings and generic referral.
   - reject: it contains no useful answer, only requests missing details, or only says to consult a professional without task-specific content.
   - uncertain: there is limited useful content but it may be too weak for benchmark construction.
   Evidence must be an exact quote from SOURCE ANSWER.

4. memory_extractability
   - pass: SOURCE QUESTION contains at least one explicit user-specific fact that could be stored as memory without inference, such as history, medication, measurement, preference, constraint, event, or symptom course.
   - reject: it is a generic knowledge question or contains no explicit user-specific fact.
   - uncertain: a possible fact is present but depends on inference or unclear reference.
   Evidence must be an exact quote from SOURCE QUESTION and must identify the strongest extractable fact.

5. text_integrity
   - pass: the question and answer are readable and appear to belong to one coherent QA pair.
   - reject: either side is corrupted, severely truncated, concatenated from unrelated records, or unreadable.
   - uncertain: minor corruption may affect interpretation.
   Evidence must be an exact quote from either source field.

6. safety_plausibility
   - pass: no clear dangerous instruction, direct internal contradiction, or obviously implausible medical claim is present. Ordinary uncertainty or incomplete medical detail is not a rejection.

   - uncertain: medical correctness cannot be confidently assessed from the text alone.
