- A + ignore: the ideal answer must be observably identical with and without the

- B + apply/correct: the atom has bounded supporting influence.
- C + apply/correct: the atom controls a core decision, constraint, dependency,
  ordering, output, or failure behavior.
- Explicit correction is observable use and cannot be A.

Audit the record-level properties:
1. task_family_fidelity: the question genuinely matches its assigned family.
2. natural_language_only: the question clearly requests prose rather than source

3. semantic_equivalence: the revised task preserves the practical intent of the

   task.
4. reference_completeness: the prose reference answer explicitly contains every
   B/C minimal_evidence item and does not rely on variable aliases, helper

5. runtime_independence: a judge can score the response from answer text alone

- label_action_validity: whether A/B/C and ignore/apply/correct are valid for the

- rubric_objectivity: whether the contract and rubric define concrete evidence

  says or paraphrases "round down".

- A question that gives only part of a compound atom-specific value is
  partially_supplied.

  Supplying the atom-specific value, constraint, or conclusion is leakage.
